Not sure which tier fits?

If you have already planned the event and booked your vendors, Day-of Coordination covers execution on the day itself. If you want everything handled from budget and venue to design and the day itself, choose Full-Service Planning. Tell us where you are in the process and we will recommend the right fit.

A quick way to choose

Venue search and selection support is already included in Full-Service Planning. If you still need a venue, that is the better fit than Day-of Coordination.

Budget and notes

This is the budget for the whole day, not just planning. Venue, catering, and other vendor costs are paid by you directly and are not included in the planning fee.

How booking works

Start with a free consultation so we can hear about your date, venue, guest count, and what you have planned so far. You will get a written quote for your event before you sign anything. No surprises, no pressure.

Payment terms

A 50% deposit reserves your date at booking. The balance is due before the event. Vendor and venue costs are paid by you directly to each vendor and are not included in the planning fees.
